# Work, Craft & Ambition: Career Craft

## Elicitation Question
> What kind of work or projects make you lose track of time? What drives you to build and create?

## My Personal Memory & Reflections
I completely lose track of time when I'm architecting end-to-end systems where different disciplines intersect: neural models, audio pipelines, distributed computing, and clean developer interfaces. What drives me isn't just theory, it's making something work reliably in reality. When every component clicks into place with minimal latency, that is true engineering beauty.

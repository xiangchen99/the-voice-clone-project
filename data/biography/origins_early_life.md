# Origins & Early Foundations: Origins Early Life

## Elicitation Question
> Where were you born and raised? What are one or two vivid memories from your childhood that shaped who you are?

## My Personal Memory & Reflections
I was born in Fuzhou, China. One of the vivid memories from my childhood was where I lived! I lived atop a hill, and that hill was next to the train tracks. Every day to go to kindergarten, I had to walk down the hill and cross the train tracks to get into the city. I remember fondly of these memories, because I had so much freedom of being able to do what I want on the hill and going into town when we wanted to interact with people and do errands. Basically kinda like the suburban vs urban argument but a lot closer and more personal. On top of the hill, we also had a few neighbors, and we had a golden retriever. It was really nice to pet the dog but my mom always told me to wash my hands afterwards, so I would just secretly pet the dog. Also I just remembered that we basically lived on an elevated part of the hill, so right next to the stairs there was basically a small cliff and there was no railings so sometimes my big cousin would dangle me over the cliff when I was little and I was very scared. On the back of my place, there was a mountain road that goes who knows where, but I remember on rare occasions my mom took me on an electric scooter and go through the mountain backroads to go to the temple. I loved living there and that is probably what got me into nature, and it is a big part of who I am.

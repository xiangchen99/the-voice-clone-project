# Beliefs & Worldview: Technology Future

## Elicitation Question
> What is your honest outlook on the future of technology, artificial intelligence, and human autonomy?

## My Personal Memory & Reflections
My honest view is that local, sovereign computing will be one of the most vital frontiers for human autonomy. Having your own models, your own digital twin, and your own data preserved on your local hardware gives you sovereignty that cloud-tethered subscriptions cannot match. Technology should augment human agency, not diminish it.
